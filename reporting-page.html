Reporting Page

Every business that sells goods has to report its sales results each quarter: the ex GST total cost including shipping, the average margin, the profit and the total value of every deal. The Reporting page gives you these figures in one place. Filter by year, account manager, financial quarter, month or company label, see the totals at a glance, then export the list of deals as a spreadsheet ready to turn into an Excel pivot table or import into your existing CRM.

At a glance

  • What it is: the sales reporting screen of QTools, a B2B quoting and CRM app for Shopify.
  • Problem solved: quarterly sales reporting that would otherwise mean exporting orders and calculating costs, margins and profit by hand.
  • Filters: year, user (account manager), financial quarter, month and company label, such as Education.
  • Totals: total value, total cost, total profit and average margin for everything that matches your filters, all ex GST and including shipping.
  • Shows per deal: order number, date, company, customer, account manager, label, total cost, margin, profit and total value.
  • Output: Export to CSV, which opens straight in Excel for pivot tables or imports into your current CRM.

Why quarterly reporting matters

Owners, finance teams and sales managers all need the same answers at the end of each quarter: how much was sold, what it cost, and how much profit it made. For a B2B business those figures must be ex GST, because GST is collected on behalf of the tax office and is not revenue, and they must include shipping, because freight is a real cost of every order that can quietly wipe out the margin on a deal.

Shopify's standard reports are built around retail orders and do not give you an ex GST cost, margin and profit for each B2B deal. Building that report by hand every quarter is slow and easy to get wrong. The Reporting page produces it for you.

Filter to exactly the report you need

The filter bar at the top of the page narrows the report down. Combine any of these:

Year
The year of the deals to include.
User
Only the deals handled by one account manager, for performance reviews and commission calculations, or all managers.
Financial quarter
A single financial quarter, for your quarterly results, or all quarters.
Month
A single month, for monthly sales meetings, or all months.
Company label
Only the deals for companies with a given label, for example Education, so you can see how each market segment is performing.

For example, you can report on the Education deals closed by one account manager in the third quarter, or every deal across the whole business for the year.

Your totals at a glance

Above the list of deals, four summary cards update as you change the filters:

Total unit price inc ship ex GST
The total value of the deals, including shipping, ex GST.
Total cost inc ship ex GST
What those deals cost your business, including shipping, ex GST.
Total profit
Total value minus total cost.
Avg margin
The average margin across the deals.

These are the four numbers most quarterly sales reports ask for, ready to copy into a board report or a management meeting.

Every deal, line by line

Below the totals, each deal that matches your filters appears as a single row:

Order
The order number of the deal.
Date
The date of the order.
Company
The company that placed the order.
Customer
The contact who placed the order.
User
The account manager responsible for the deal.
Label
The company's label, such as Education or Government.
Total cost inc ship ex GST
What the deal cost your business, including shipping, ex GST.
Margin
The deal's profit as a percentage of its total value.
Profit
The deal's total value minus its total cost.
Total unit price inc ship ex GST
The value of the deal, including shipping, ex GST.

Export to Excel for pivot tables and your CRM

The Export to CSV button downloads the filtered report as a spreadsheet that opens straight in Excel, with one row per deal and one column per field. That is exactly the layout a pivot table needs, so in a few clicks you can total profit by account manager, compare margins across company labels or chart revenue by month.

The same file imports cleanly into the CRM or accounting system your business already uses, so QTools can sit alongside your existing tools rather than replace them.

Part of the QTools sales workflow

The report is built from the deals you quote on the Deal Edit page and move through the pipeline on the Deals page, using the unit costs from the Product Catalog page and the labels you give companies on the Companies page.
