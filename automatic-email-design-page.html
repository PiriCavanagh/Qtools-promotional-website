Automatic Email Design Page

The Automatic Email Design page sets up the emails QTools sends your customers automatically. When a customer places an order, their accounts team receives a thank you for your order email with the business invoice attached as a PDF, followed by an automatic payment reminder if it is not paid by the due date. When a customer requests a quote through your store's self serve quote process, they receive an email with the quote PDF attached. This is the workflow business customers expect: the document itself in their inbox, not a link they have to click to download it.

At a glance

  • What it is: the automatic email screen of QTools, a B2B quoting and CRM app for Shopify.
  • Invoice email: a thank you for your order email with the business invoice PDF attached, sent to the company's accounts email when an order is placed or a deal is marked Won.
  • Payment reminder: the invoice email is sent again if the order is not marked paid by its due date.
  • Quote email: sent when a customer requests a quote through your Shopify store's self serve quote process, with the quote PDF attached.
  • Designer: set the subject line, preview text, from name and reply to address, and build the email body from a banner or logo, hero image and text, line items table, body text and footer, with a live preview.
  • Key difference: the PDF is attached to the email, instead of the download link most other apps send.
  • Why it matters: business customers can open, forward and file the document straight away, without clicking an external link that their company may block or hold for scanning.

The invoice email: thank you, with the invoice attached

As soon as a customer places an order, or your sales representative marks a deal as Won, QTools sends a thank you for your order email with the business invoice attached as a PDF. It goes straight to the company's accounts email, so the people who pay the bills have everything they need the moment it arrives, with no need to log in to your store or request a copy of the invoice.

This matters most for B2B orders placed on a credit account or paid by bank transfer, where the invoice is what triggers payment. The faster it reaches the right person, the faster you are paid.

Automatic payment reminders

If an order has not been marked paid by its due date, QTools sends the invoice email again as a payment reminder, with the invoice still attached. Your team no longer has to track overdue invoices and chase them by hand, and the customer's accounts team receives the document they need to pay, not just a request for payment.

The quote email: self serve quotes, with the PDF attached

Many business buyers need a formal quote before they can raise a purchase order. With a self serve quote process on your Shopify store, customers can request a quote themselves, at any time, without waiting for a sales representative. QTools then emails them the quote with the quote PDF attached, ready to forward to their manager or procurement team for approval.

Your store keeps quoting while your team is busy or out of the office, and every self serve quote reaches the customer as a proper document rather than a link.

Design the email around the PDF

The PDF attached to each email comes from your templates on the Quote/Invoice Design page. The Automatic Email Design page controls the email wrapped around it, in two groups of settings:

Inbox content

Subject line
The email's subject, for example "Invoice {{quoteName}} — payment due {{dueDate}}".
Preview text
The short line shown after the subject in most inboxes, for example "Your invoice from {{companyName}} is attached."
From name
The sender name your customer sees, such as Acme Billing.
Reply to address
Where replies go, such as your accounts email address.

Placeholders such as {{companyName}}, {{quoteName}} and {{dueDate}} work in the subject line and in any body text, and are filled in with each customer's details when the email is sent.

Email body

Banner / logo
Your logo from the Quote/Invoice Design settings, or your company name as text if the logo is turned off or not set.
Hero image + text
An image across the top of the email with text over it, such as "Thanks for your order".
Line items table
The items on the order, with quantity, line totals and the order total.
Body text
Your message to the customer, for example asking them to keep the invoice for warranty purposes.
Footer
Your contact details and locations.

A live preview, filled with sample data, shows exactly how the email will look as you edit it. Press Save when you are happy with it.

Why attachments, not download links

Many quoting and invoicing apps email a link to download the document instead of the document itself. That works for consumers, but it creates real problems for business customers:

Links are often blocked
Many companies do not allow employees to open certain links from outside sources, so the person who needs the quote or invoice may not be able to reach it at all.
Links are held for scanning
Company antivirus and email security systems investigate external links closely, rewriting, sandboxing or holding them before they can be opened. This can stall a quote or invoice for hours, or stop it completely.
Links train people to click
Phishing attacks commonly imitate "click here to view your invoice" emails. A business customer who receives the PDF from a supplier they are expecting to hear from does not need to click an unfamiliar link to see it.
Links do not travel well
A quote usually has to be forwarded to a manager or procurement team, and an invoice filed with accounts. An attached PDF can be forwarded, saved and filed as it is. A link may expire, require a login or stop working once it leaves the original inbox.

With the PDF attached, your quote or invoice arrives as a complete document that your customer can open, forward and act on straight away.

Part of the QTools sales workflow

The PDFs attached to these emails are the quote and invoice templates you design on the Quote/Invoice Design page, so every automatic email carries the same branded, professional documents as the quotes your team builds on the Deal Edit page.
