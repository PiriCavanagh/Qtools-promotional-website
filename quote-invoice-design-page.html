Quote/Invoice Design Page

The Quote/Invoice Design page is where you design how your quote and invoice PDFs look and what information they show. It uses an intuitive drag and drop block builder, similar to the Shopify theme editor you already use for your online store. Drop content blocks into the header, body and footer, arrange them into columns with layout blocks, and style everything with familiar settings like margin, padding, font size, colour, borders and border radius.

At a glance

  • What it is: the PDF template designer of QTools, a B2B quoting and CRM app for Shopify.
  • Designs: separate templates for quotes and invoices, switched with the Quote and Invoice tabs.
  • How it works: a drag and drop block builder, similar to the Shopify theme editor.
  • Three areas: header, body and footer, each holding its own blocks.
  • Two block types: content blocks for quote information, and layout blocks that arrange content blocks into columns.
  • Styling: margin, padding, font size, hex colour codes, borders and border radius.
  • Live preview: every change appears on the PDF preview straight away.

Why your quote design matters

A quote is often the first formal document a business customer sees from you, and it is usually forwarded to a manager or procurement team before it is approved. A clear, professional, branded quote builds trust and makes it easy for the approver to find what they need: who the quote is for, what is included, the ex GST prices, the totals, the payment details and your terms.

Every business also needs different information on its documents. One may need a customer reference number and contract number, another bank details for EFT payment, another detailed terms and conditions. Fixed templates force you to compromise. The Quote/Invoice Design page lets you decide exactly what appears and where.

Header, body and footer

Each template is split into three areas, and you drag blocks into whichever area they belong in:

Header
The top of the document, typically your logo alongside the quote or invoice number.
Body
The main content: quote details, billing and delivery addresses, payment details, line items, totals, payment instructions and terms.
Footer
The bottom of the document, for details such as your company information or contact details.

Drag the handle on any block to reorder it, press + Add Section to add a new block, use the eye icon to show or hide a block without deleting it, and use the bin icon to remove it.

Content blocks

Content blocks hold the information on your quote or invoice. Some have a predefined layout for typical quote information, and others let you create your own:

Details Grid
The key quote details laid out in a grid, such as quote name, customer reference number, customer, company, date issued, expiry date, account manager and contract.
Invoice Details
Billing, address and payment details, including payment status and payment terms.
Line Items
The products on the quote, with image, title, SKU, quantity, ex GST price and line total, plus a link to each product's info page.
Totals
The subtotal, shipping, GST and total.
Image
Your logo or any other image.
Custom Text
Any text you choose, such as EFT payment details, a heading, or your quote terms and conditions.
Line
A dividing line to separate sections of the document.

Layout blocks for columns

Layout blocks, such as Double Column and Triple Column, split the page into columns. Drop content blocks into each column to place information side by side across the page. For example, a Double Column in the header can hold your logo on the left and the quote number on the right, a Triple Column in the body can show billing, address and payment details next to each other, and another Double Column can place your EFT payment details beside your terms and conditions.

This is how a quote goes from a long single column of information to a compact, professional document that is easy to read at a glance.

Style it with familiar web settings

Select any area or block to edit its properties. The builder uses the same styling settings as a web page, so anyone who has edited a website will feel at home:

Margin
The space around the outside of a block.
Padding
The space between a block's edge and its content.
Font size
The size of the text in a block.
Colour
Any colour, entered as a hex code such as #1F2A8C to match your brand exactly.
Borders
Lines around a block.
Border radius
How rounded a block's corners are.

The Template Settings panel also sets an accent colour for the whole template, so your headings and highlights match your brand in one step.

See every change as you make it

The centre of the page shows a live preview of your PDF, filled with sample quote information. Every block you add, move or restyle appears on the preview straight away, so you can see exactly what your customer will receive. When you are happy with the design, press Save template.

The templates you design here are used for every quote you create on the Deal Edit page, filled with the products, prices and customer details from that deal.
