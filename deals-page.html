Deals Page

The Deals page is the CRM pipeline of QTools. It shows every open deal in a familiar deal stages view inside the Shopify admin, so you can see at a glance where each quote is up to, how much it is worth and how much revenue your pipeline is likely to deliver.

At a glance

  • What it is: the CRM deal pipeline of QTools, a B2B quoting and CRM app for Shopify.
  • Best for: small B2B stores that mostly sell online but still need core CRM and quoting features for the occasional quote request.
  • Stages: five deal stages, each of which can be renamed.
  • Forecasting: every stage shows its total value, deal count and weighted expected revenue.
  • Views: Pipeline View, List View and Graph View against your sales targets.
  • Tools: one search across deals, leads, customers and companies, quick add, and filters by sales representative or custom criteria.

Who the Deals page is for

Many B2B businesses on Shopify make most of their sales through the online store, yet still receive requests for formal quotes from business customers. A dedicated CRM is often more than these stores need. QTools provides the fundamental CRM and quoting features inside the Shopify admin, so the occasional quote can be tracked from first enquiry to closed sale without paying for or maintaining a separate system.

Five customisable deal stages

The Pipeline View arranges deals into five stages, from the first enquiry through to the final negotiation. Each stage can be renamed to suit the way your business sells. The stages in the screenshot above are:

  1. Prospecting / lead in

    New enquiries and early opportunities. Weighted at 10% in the example.

  2. Qualified, defining bill of materials

    The customer's requirements are confirmed and the list of products is being worked out. Weighted at 25%.

  3. Deal issued

    The quote has been sent to the customer. Weighted at 50%.

  4. Updating / revising

    The quote is being adjusted in response to customer feedback. Weighted at 60%.

  5. Final negotiations

    The deal is close to being won. Weighted at 80%.

Each deal appears as a card showing its quote number, the customer company and location, how many days old the deal is, and its total value. Opening a card takes you to the Deal Edit page for that quote.

Stage totals and weighted expected revenue

The top of every stage shows three figures:

Total value
The combined value of every deal currently in that stage.
Number of deals
How many deals are in that stage.
Weighted expected revenue
The total value multiplied by the stage's weighting, which reflects how likely deals at that stage are to close.

For example, if a stage holds one deal worth $100 and that stage has a 10% weighting, the expected revenue for the stage is $10. In the screenshot, the Prospecting stage holds two deals worth a combined $8,522, which at a 10% weighting gives an expected revenue of $852. Adding the weighted figures across all stages gives a realistic forecast of the revenue your pipeline is likely to produce, rather than an optimistic total of every open quote.

Pipeline, list and graph views

Three buttons at the top left switch between views of the same deals:

Pipeline View
The deal stages view described above, ideal for tracking progress and forecasting.
List View
A conventional list of every deal without stages, for businesses that do not need a staged sales process.
Graph View
Charts built from the data in all your deals, measured against your sales targets.

Search, quick add and filters

Global search
The main search field on the Deals page searches every deal, lead, customer and company, across both your Shopify store and the QTools database.
Quick add
The plus button beside the search field creates a new deal, lead, customer or company in one click.
Owner filter
Filters the pipeline by user, meaning the sales representative who owns each deal, so each representative can focus on their own deals and managers can review individual performance.
Deal filter builder
Builds more detailed filters for finding exactly the deals you need.

A CRM sales pipeline inside Shopify

The Deals page shows every deal in a five stage pipeline inside the Shopify admin, with the total value, deal count and weighted expected revenue for each stage.

Renameable deal stages

All five stages can be renamed to match how your business sells.

How expected revenue is calculated

QTools multiplies the total value of the deals in each stage by that stage's weighting. One $100 deal in a stage weighted at 10% gives an expected revenue of $10.

List and graph views

Switch between Pipeline View, List View and Graph View at any time.
