Product Catalog Page

The Product Catalog page shows every product in your Shopify store in an easily digestible list with its image, supplier, SKU, unit cost, unit price and margin. It is also where you set must have and recommended product pairings, so your sales team can never quote an incomplete solution, and where you track true sales per product, including orders paid on credit account or by bank transfer.

At a glance

  • What it is: the product catalogue screen of QTools, a B2B quoting and CRM app for Shopify.
  • Shows: image, title, supplier, SKU, unit cost, unit price, default margin, pairings and total sales for every product.
  • Key feature: must have and recommended product pairings that are enforced when quoting.
  • Why it matters: stops incomplete solutions, such as a device without its power supply, being proposed to a customer.
  • Sales tracking: sales per product across card payments, credit accounts with payment terms and bank transfers.
  • Tools: search by title or SKU, export the catalogue to Excel and copy a link to each product's info page.

Your whole catalogue, with costs and margins

Each product appears as a single row showing:

Image
The product's main image from Shopify, for quick visual identification.
Title
The product name.
Supplier
The supplier the product is purchased from.
SKU
The product's stock keeping unit code.
Unit cost
What the product costs your business to buy.
Unit price
What the product sells for.
Default margin
The margin between the product's unit cost and unit price.
Must have products
The products that must be quoted alongside this one, added with the plus button.
Recommended products
The products suggested alongside this one, added with the plus button.
Total sales
How many units of the product have sold, across every payment method.
Product info page
A Copy link button that copies the link to the product's information page, ready to paste into an email to a customer.

Seeing cost, price and margin side by side for every product makes it easy to spot items that are priced too low, check supplier costs and keep pricing consistent before any quote is written.

Search and export

A search field at the top of the page finds any product by title or SKU. The Export to Excel button downloads the catalogue, including costs, prices and margins, as a spreadsheet for pricing reviews or supplier negotiations.

Must have and recommended product pairings

The Must have products and Recommended products columns let you attach other products to any product in two ways, using the plus button in each row:

Must have products
Products that are required for the main product to work. When a sales representative adds the main product to a quote, QTools requires them to add its must have products as well.
Recommended products
Products that complement the main product. Once the must have products are added, QTools suggests the recommended products, so the representative can offer them to the customer.

Many business products cannot function on their own. A display may need a power supply, a projector may need a mount, and a network device may need specific cables or licences. Without pairings, it is easy for a busy sales representative to leave a required item off a quote. The customer then receives an incomplete solution, and the missing item has to be sold separately later, causing delays, extra shipping and frustration.

Must have pairings prevent this. Every quote that includes a product automatically includes everything that product needs, so customers always receive a complete, working solution and your team avoids costly follow up orders.

True sales per product, including credit accounts and bank transfers

B2B customers often do not pay by credit card. They may buy on a credit account with payment terms, such as 30 days, or pay by bank transfer. These payments never pass through Shopify's inbuilt sales calculations, so the standard Shopify reports can understate how well a product is really selling to business customers.

The Product Catalog page monitors sales per product across every payment method: card payments, credit account orders with payment terms and bank transfers. This gives a complete picture of which products drive your B2B revenue.

Part of the QTools sales workflow

The product catalogue feeds every quote. Products added with Add Product on the Deal Edit page bring their cost, price, margin and pairings with them, and new one off items created there appear in the catalogue too. Accepted quotes then move through the pipeline on the Deals page.
