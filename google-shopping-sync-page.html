Google Shopping Sync Page

Business buyers expect to see prices ex GST, but Google Shopping requires tax inclusive prices in most countries. The Google Shopping Sync page solves this conflict. You keep your Shopify store priced ex GST for your B2B customers, and QTools adds your country's tax rate to each price and posts the tax inclusive price to Google using your Merchant ID, Data Source ID and a Google Cloud service account.

At a glance

  • What it is: the Google Shopping sync screen of QTools, a B2B quoting and CRM app for Shopify.
  • Problem solved: B2B stores price ex GST, but Google Shopping rejects or misrepresents prices that do not include tax.
  • How it works: QTools adds the GST or VAT rate for your country to each ex GST price before posting it to Google.
  • Countries: Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom and Germany, where Google requires tax inclusive prices. The United States is the exception, and prices are sent without tax.
  • Connection: your Google Merchant ID, Data Source ID, service account email and private key.
  • Syncing: sync on demand with Sync now, or turn on hourly sync to keep Google up to date automatically.
  • Why it matters: your store stays ex GST for business buyers, while your Google listings stay compliant and your products stay visible.

Why B2B stores price ex GST

Registered businesses do not carry the cost of goods and services tax. They pay it at checkout and then claim it back from the tax office, so the price that matters to them is the price before tax. Procurement teams compare suppliers, set budgets and approve purchase orders using ex GST figures, and procurement platforms display prices the same way.

A store that shows tax inclusive prices forces every business buyer to do the arithmetic themselves, and makes your products look more expensive than a competitor that shows ex GST prices. For a B2B store, ex GST pricing is the standard.

Why Google Shopping needs tax inclusive prices

Google Shopping is built for consumers, who do pay the tax. In Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom and Germany, Google requires the price in your product listing to include GST or VAT, so shoppers see what they will actually pay. If the price Google finds on your listing does not match the tax inclusive price it expects, your products can be flagged for a price mismatch and disapproved.

The United States is the exception. There, Google expects prices without sales tax, because sales tax varies by state and is calculated at checkout.

Without a sync tool, a B2B store has two bad options: switch the whole store to tax inclusive pricing and frustrate business buyers, or leave Google Shopping out of the sales mix entirely.

How the sync works

QTools reads the ex GST price of each product from your Shopify store, applies the tax rate for your current country, taken from Settings → Tax in QTools, and posts the resulting tax inclusive price to your Google Merchant Center account. Your Shopify prices are never changed.

Country Tax Price in Shopify Price sent to Google
Australia GST 10% $100.00 ex GST $110.00
Canada GST 5% $100.00 ex GST $105.00
United Kingdom VAT 20% £100.00 ex VAT £120.00
Germany VAT (MwSt) 19% €100.00 net €119.00
United States No tax added $100.00 $100.00

Business buyers visiting your store see the ex GST price they expect, and shoppers who find you on Google see the tax inclusive price Google requires.

Connecting to Google Merchant Center

The sync posts prices directly to your Google Merchant Center account through a Google Cloud service account. You enter four details once in the Merchant Center connection panel and press Save connection:

Merchant ID
The ID of your Google Merchant Center account, shown in the top corner of Merchant Center.
Data source ID
The ID of the product data source in Merchant Center that QTools posts your products and prices to.
Service account email
The email address of the Google Cloud service account that has access to your Merchant Center account.
Service account private key
The private_key value from the service account's JSON key file, which authorises QTools to update your product data.

Sync now or every hour

Press Sync now to push every eligible product to Google straight away. Turn on Enable hourly sync and QTools updates Google automatically every hour, so price changes you make in Shopify reach your Google listings without any manual work.
