Companies Page

The Companies page is a single dashboard for every B2B company your Shopify store sells to. It shows each company's labels, open quotes, won and lost deals, locations, total spend and account manager at a glance, and lets you label companies by sector so you can instantly filter to, for example, every education customer or every government organisation.

At a glance

  • What it is: the B2B company dashboard of QTools, a B2B quoting and CRM app for Shopify.
  • Shows: name, labels, open quotes, won deals, lost deals, locations, total spent and assigned user.
  • Key feature: custom labels on every company, with filtering by label, such as education or government.
  • Actions: assign a sales representative, edit company details, search, filter and add new companies.
  • Data source: your existing Shopify B2B Companies.

Label and segment your companies

The most important feature of the Companies page is the Labels column. Any number of labels can be added to each company using the plus button in its row, and the list can then be filtered by label to show only the companies that match.

Labels let a business group its customers in whatever way suits its market. A supplier might label companies as education, government, healthcare or corporate, then filter to show all education companies ahead of the school year, or all government organisations when a new standing offer arrangement is announced. Segmenting companies this way makes targeted follow up, reporting and account planning straightforward, without exporting data to a spreadsheet or a separate CRM.

Every company's key figures in one row

Each company appears as a single row showing:

Name
The company's name, taken from its Shopify B2B Company record.
Labels
The custom labels assigned to the company, used for grouping and filtering.
Open quotes
How many quotes are currently open for the company.
Won deals
How many deals with the company have been won.
Lost deals
How many deals with the company have been lost.
Locations
How many locations the company has, such as separate offices, campuses or sites.
Total spent
The total value the company has spent with your business.
User
The sales representative who manages the account, which can be changed directly from the row.
Edit
Opens the company's details for editing.

Seeing open quotes alongside won and lost deals shows how each account is performing. A company with many lost deals may need a different approach, while a company with high total spend and several open quotes is a clear priority for the sales team.

Search, filter and add companies

A search field at the top of the page finds any company by name. The Filters button narrows the list further, and the Labels and User columns can each be filtered, so a sales representative can see only their own accounts or only the companies with a particular label. The New company button adds a company without leaving QTools.

Part of the QTools sales workflow

Companies connect every other part of QTools. Each company's contacts appear on the Customers page, its deals move through the pipeline on the Deals page, and every quote for it is built on the Deal Edit page, where the company and location are the first step of the guided setup.
