Customers Page

The Customers page is a B2B customer dashboard inside the Shopify admin. It shows every customer's contact details, company, role, account manager, location, open quotes, orders and total spend in one place, and removes the direct to consumer information that clutters the standard Shopify customers page.

At a glance

  • What it is: the B2B customer dashboard of QTools, a B2B quoting and CRM app for Shopify.
  • Shows: name, email, phone, company, role, managing user, location, open quotes, orders and amount spent.
  • Actions: view a customer's current deals, or edit their name, email, phone, company and role.
  • Hides: tags, notes, timeline, store credit and marketing status, which are direct to consumer features.
  • Data source: your existing Shopify customers and companies.

Why a dedicated B2B customer view

The customers dashboard supplied by Shopify is designed for direct to consumer (DTC) retail. It gives prominent space to tags, notes, the customer timeline, store credit and email marketing status. These features suit a shop selling to the public, but in a business to business context they add clutter and push the information a sales team actually needs out of view.

The QTools Customers page strips those DTC features away. What remains is a simple dashboard focused on who each customer is, which company they buy for, who looks after them and how much business they bring in.

Everything about a customer in one row

Each customer appears as a single row showing:

Name
The customer's full name.
Email and phone
Their contact details, ready for a follow up call or email.
Assigned company
The Shopify B2B Company the customer buys on behalf of.
Role
Their Shopify B2B role within that company, such as Ordering only, which controls what they can do when buying on the company's behalf.
Managing user
The member of your sales team who manages the relationship with this customer.
Location
The company location the customer belongs to.
Open quotes
How many quotes are currently open for the customer.
Orders
How many orders the customer has placed.
Amount spent
The total value the customer has spent with your business.

Search, filter and add customers

A search field at the top of the page finds any customer by name or email address, and the Filters button narrows the list further, for example to the customers managed by one sales representative. The New customer button adds a customer without leaving QTools.

View a customer's deals

Every customer row has a View deals button. It shows the customer's current deals straight away, so a sales representative can check what has already been quoted before picking up the phone. Each deal opens on the Deal Edit page, and every deal also appears in the pipeline on the Deals page.

Edit customer details in a simple dialog

The edit icon on each row opens a simple dialog for updating the customer's name, email, phone, company and role. Details can be kept up to date in seconds, without opening the full Shopify customer record.

Part of the QTools sales workflow

The Customers page works alongside the rest of QTools. New enquiries are tracked through the pipeline on the Deals page and quoted on the Deal Edit page, while the Customers page keeps a clear record of every business contact and the value of their account.
