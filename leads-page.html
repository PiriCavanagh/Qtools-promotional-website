Leads Page

The Leads page is where QTools users record and follow up new sales enquiries inside the Shopify admin. Each lead is a simple entry with a title, a lead type, where it came from, the next planned action and the person responsible, giving small businesses a clear list of every opportunity without the cost or complexity of a full CRM platform.

At a glance

  • What it is: the lead tracking screen of QTools, a B2B quoting and CRM app for Shopify.
  • Best for: small companies that receive a few leads a week and do not need a complete CRM platform.
  • Lead fields: title, label (lead type), source origin, next activity and owner.
  • Search: one search field that matches across every lead field.
  • Where it lives: inside the Shopify admin, alongside your deals, customers and companies.

Who the Leads page is for

Most small B2B businesses on Shopify receive only a handful of new enquiries each week. Those leads still need to be recorded and followed up, but a full CRM platform brings extra cost, extra logins and far more features than a small team will use. The QTools Leads page is a simple value add: a lightweight place to capture each enquiry and plan the next step, built into the Shopify admin your team already works in every day.

What each lead records

Creating a lead takes only a few seconds. Every lead is made up of five fields:

Title
A short name for the lead, such as the company or the project the enquiry relates to.
Label
The lead type, used to group similar leads together, for example new business, repeat customer or tender.
Source origin
Where the lead came from, such as email, phone, the website, a referral or a trade show. Recording the source shows which channels bring in the most enquiries.
Next activity
A short note describing the next planned action, such as calling back on Monday or sending a product catalogue, so nothing is forgotten.
Owner
The user currently responsible for the lead, so everyone on the team knows who is following it up.

Search across every field

The search field at the top of the Leads page matches against every lead field: title, label, source origin, next activity and owner. A sales representative can find all of their own leads, every lead that arrived by phone, or every lead of a particular type in one search.

From lead to deal

Leads sit alongside the rest of the QTools sales workflow. When an enquiry becomes a real opportunity, it can be progressed on the Deals page and quoted on the Deal Edit page, all without leaving the Shopify admin.
