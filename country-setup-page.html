Country setup

Country Setup Page

QTools supports B2B stores in Australia, Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany and the United States. Each country has its own goods and services tax or VAT rate, its own rules for how prices must appear on Google Shopping, and its own financial year. This page explains what changes in each country, when you need the QTools Google Shopping Sync page, and how to set your financial year so the Reporting page groups your deals into the right quarters.

At a glance

Every business can choose its own financial year, so the common financial years above are a starting point. You set the financial year your business actually uses in QTools, and the Reporting page uses it for its financial quarter filter.

Australia

GST

Australia charges a goods and services tax (GST) of 10% on most goods and services. Registered businesses claim back the GST they pay, which is why business buyers compare prices ex GST.

Google Shopping

Google requires prices in Australian Shopping listings to include GST. Use the Google Shopping Sync page to keep your store priced ex GST while QTools adds the 10% GST to the prices it sends to Google.

Financial year quarters

The Australian financial year runs from 1 July to 30 June:

Canada

GST, HST, PST and QST

Canada's tax rate depends on the province or territory the goods are delivered to:

GST 5%
The federal goods and services tax, charged everywhere in Canada. In Alberta and the territories it is the only sales tax.
HST 13% to 15%
The harmonized sales tax, which combines GST and provincial tax: 13% in Ontario, 14% in Nova Scotia, and 15% in New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, and Prince Edward Island.
GST + PST
5% GST plus a separate provincial sales tax in British Columbia (7%), Manitoba (7%) and Saskatchewan (6%).
GST + QST
5% GST plus the 9.975% Quebec sales tax.

Google Shopping

Use the Google Shopping Sync page to keep your store priced before tax while QTools sends your prices to Google.

Financial year quarters

The Government of Canada's fiscal year runs from 1 April to 31 March, and many Canadian businesses use the calendar year instead. For an April to March financial year:

Q1
April, May, June
Q2
July, August, September
Q3
October, November, December
Q4
January, February, March

United Kingdom

VAT

The United Kingdom charges value added tax (VAT) at a standard rate of 20%. Some goods have a reduced rate of 5%, such as children's car seats and home energy, or a zero rate of 0%, such as most food and children's clothing. VAT registered businesses reclaim the VAT they pay, so B2B buyers compare prices ex VAT.

Google Shopping

Google requires prices in UK Shopping listings to include VAT. Use the Google Shopping Sync page to keep your store priced ex VAT while QTools adds the 20% VAT to the prices it sends to Google.

Financial year quarters

The UK government's financial year runs from 1 April to 31 March, and many UK businesses align with it:

Q1
April, May, June
Q2
July, August, September
Q3
October, November, December
Q4
January, February, March

Germany

VAT (Umsatzsteuer)

Germany charges VAT, known as Umsatzsteuer or Mehrwertsteuer (MwSt), at a standard rate of 19%. A reduced rate of 7% applies to some goods, such as most food and books. Businesses reclaim the VAT they pay as input tax (Vorsteuer), so B2B prices are quoted net of VAT.

Google Shopping

Google requires prices in German Shopping listings to include VAT. Use the Google Shopping Sync page to keep your store priced net of VAT while QTools adds the 19% VAT to the prices it sends to Google.

Financial year quarters

Most German businesses use the calendar year as their financial year (Geschäftsjahr):

Q1
January, February, March
Q2
April, May, June
Q3
July, August, September
Q4
October, November, December

United States

Sales tax

The United States has no national GST or VAT. Instead, most states and many cities and counties charge their own sales tax, calculated at checkout based on where the order is delivered. Alaska, Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon have no statewide sales tax.

Google Shopping

Google expects prices in US Shopping listings to exclude sales tax, which matches the prices already in a B2B store. You do not need the QTools Google Shopping Sync page in the United States. Use the default Google product feed instead, such as Shopify's Google & YouTube sales channel.

Financial year quarters

Most US businesses use the calendar year as their financial year, while the US federal government's fiscal year runs from 1 October to 30 September. For a calendar financial year:

Q1
January, February, March
Q2
April, May, June
Q3
July, August, September
Q4
October, November, December

Set your financial year in QTools

Quarterly sales results only make sense if your quarters match your financial year. You set your financial year in QTools, and the financial quarter filter on the Reporting page follows it. Choose Q1, for example, and the report shows the deals from the first three months of your financial year, whether that is July to September in Australia, April to June in the United Kingdom or January to March in Germany.
